# Kimmy Home — downloads

Kimmy Home receives backups from **Kimmy** on your iPhone, directly over your home Wi-Fi.
Your photos go from your iPhone to your own computer and nowhere else: no cloud, no account.

## Download

Get the latest version from the **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** page:

- **Windows:** `Kimmy-Home-<version>-setup.exe` — open it and follow the steps. No administrator password needed.
- **Mac:** `Kimmy-Home-<version>.dmg` — open it and drag Kimmy Home into Applications.
  Until Kimmy Home is signed with an Apple Developer certificate, macOS may say it "can't be opened":
  right-click (or Control-click) Kimmy Home, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. This is needed once.

Then open Kimmy on your iPhone, go to **Backup**, and scan the QR code Kimmy Home shows.

## Updates

Kimmy Home checks for new versions by itself and shows an **Update** button when there is one.
The check asks this page for the version number only; nothing about you or your photos is sent.
