# Kimmy privacy policy

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

Kimmy is a photo organiser and backup tool for iPhone, with Kimmy Home for Mac and Windows. It is built on one rule: **your photos stay with you.** There is no Kimmy account, no Kimmy cloud and no Kimmy server that ever sees your photos.

## What Kimmy does with your photos

- **On your iPhone only.** Kimmy reads the photos and videos on your iPhone to find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots, large videos and other clutter, and to sort your library by time, place and app. All of this runs on the iPhone.
- **Looking inside photos.** When you start it in the Library, Kimmy uses the iPhone's own recognition to find people and things. It runs on the iPhone.
- **Faces.** Only if you allow it, Kimmy compares faces to group photos by person. Face data stays on your iPhone, is never shared, and is used only to group your photos. You can erase it at any time in Library › People.
- **Place names.** To name the places in your Library, Kimmy asks Apple Maps for the name of each area. Only a rough position (about 20 km) is sent, never your photos.
- **Deleting.** Nothing is deleted until you empty Kimmy's Bin and confirm on your iPhone. Deleted items stay in the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days.
- **The vault.** Photos you move to the vault are encrypted and kept only inside Kimmy on your iPhone. The key never leaves your iPhone. If you choose a vault with your own password and forget it, nobody, including us, can open it.

## Kimmy Direct (backup to your own computer)

- Kimmy backs up to **your** computer, NAS or drive: to a folder you choose, or to Kimmy Home on your Mac or PC, directly over your home network. Backups go from your iPhone to your computer and nowhere else. They work with no internet connection.
- Kimmy Home keeps your photos as ordinary files in the folder you chose. You can open, move or delete them with your computer's own tools at any time.
- "Back up when I arrive home" (optional) uses your iPhone's location to notice when you get home. The location stays on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

## What leaves your device

- **Nothing about your photos.** Not the photos, not previews, not names, dates or places.
- **Purchases.** Kimmy Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and tells Kimmy only whether your subscription is active. We never see your payment details.
- **Updates.** Kimmy Home asks our update page for the latest version number now and then. Nothing about you or your photos is sent with that request.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.** Kimmy contains no analytics or advertising code, and we collect no usage data.

## Your control

- "Erase all Kimmy data" in Settings removes everything Kimmy saved on your iPhone: its scan, your bin list, names, face data and settings. Your photos are not touched. Deleting the app does the same.
- Kimmy Home's data (the list of paired iPhones and its settings) lives on your computer. Uninstalling Kimmy Home removes the program; your backed-up photos stay in your folder.

## Children

Kimmy is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the app will point to it.
